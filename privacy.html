Privacy Policy


Effective date: October 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tolga Cebeci, trading as Revolave Studio ("Revolave", "we", "us" or "our"), collects, uses and shares information when you use any game or application we publish on Google Play, the Apple App Store or any other store (each an "App"). Revolave is the data controller for the information described here, including for the purposes of the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR") and Turkish Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data ("KVKK"). Terms not defined here have the meaning given in our End User License Agreement.


1. Information We Collect


We do not ask you for your name, email address or phone number to play our Apps. The Apps, and the third-party services built into them, collect the following information automatically:

· Device identifiers: the advertising identifier of your device (Google Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iOS, the latter only if you allow tracking), the identifier for vendor (IDFV) on iOS, app-specific installation identifiers, and identifiers assigned by our analytics and advertising partners.

· Device and network information: device model, operating system and version, language, time zone, country, mobile carrier, screen size, app version and IP address. Your approximate location (country or city) may be derived from your IP address. We do not collect precise GPS location.

· Gameplay and usage information: when and how long you play, levels started and completed, scores, progress, in-game events, settings, and how you interact with advertisements.

· Purchase information: for in-app purchases, the product bought, price, currency, transaction identifier and purchase status. Payments are processed by Google or Apple; we never receive your card or bank details.

· Diagnostic information: crash reports, error logs and performance data.

· Attribution information: information about the advertising campaign through which you found and installed the App.

· Account and cloud save information (some Apps only): if you sign in with Google Play Games or Apple Game Center, we receive your player identifier and player name from that service, and your game progress is stored with Google (Google Play Games, Google Cloud) or Apple (Game Center, iCloud). We do not receive your Google or Apple password or email address.

· Support communications: if you email us, your email address and the contents of your message.

The Apps do not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or files.


2. How We Use Information


· To provide the App, save and restore your progress and deliver in-app purchases;

· To understand how the App is played, fix bugs and improve game design and performance;

· To measure the performance of our marketing campaigns and attribute installs;

· To show advertisements, including personalized advertisements where you have consented, and to cap how often you see them;

· To detect and prevent fraud, cheating and abuse;

· To answer your support requests; and

· To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.


3. Legal Bases


Where GDPR or KVKK applies, we process information on these bases: performance of our contract with you (providing the App, cloud saves and purchases); your consent (personalized advertising, tracking across other companies' apps and websites, and non-essential analytics where consent is required); our legitimate interests (keeping the App secure, fixing errors, basic analytics and non-personalized advertising, which funds our free Apps); and compliance with legal obligations. You can withdraw consent at any time as described in section 5; this does not affect processing carried out before the withdrawal.


4. Third-Party Services


The Apps use the following third-party services, which receive the information described above for the purposes listed. Some of them act on our behalf; others, in particular advertising and measurement partners, also use the information for their own purposes under their own privacy policies:

· Voodoo (TinySauce SDK): publishing partner; receives gameplay, analytics and campaign data to evaluate and improve the game.

· GameAnalytics: gameplay analytics.

· Adjust: install attribution, campaign measurement and fraud prevention.

· Meta (Facebook SDK): app event measurement and advertising campaign optimization.

· Google AdMob: displaying advertisements, together with the ad partners AdMob works with, such as Meta Audience Network.

· Google (Google Play Services, Google Play Games, Google Play Billing, Google Cloud and Firebase): sign-in, cloud saves, purchases and app infrastructure.

· Apple (App Store, Game Center, iCloud): sign-in, cloud saves and purchases.

· Unity: game engine, which may collect diagnostic and performance data.

We do not sell your personal information for money. We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights or users' safety, or to a successor if our business or an App is transferred.


5. Advertising, Tracking and Your Choices


Our Apps are free and supported by advertising. Ads may be personalized (based on your device identifiers and activity in our Apps and other apps) or non-personalized (based on context such as the App and your approximate location).

· EEA, UK and Switzerland: before personalized ads or non-essential tracking are used, the App asks for your consent through a consent message. You can change your choice through the privacy settings option in the App, where provided, or by contacting us.

· iOS: we only access your IDFA and track you across other companies' apps and websites if you allow it in the App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

· Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

If you opt out, you will still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you.


6. Data Retention


We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above. Game data stored on your device is removed when you uninstall the App. Cloud save data is kept until you delete it or ask us to delete it. Analytics, attribution and advertising data is kept by our partners for the periods set out in their privacy policies, after which it is deleted or anonymized. Support emails are kept for up to two years after your request is resolved. We may keep information longer where the law requires it.


7. Your Rights and Data Deletion


Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict its processing, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising. Under Article 11 of KVKK you also have the right to learn whether your data is processed, its purpose, and the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom it is transferred, and to claim compensation for unlawful processing.

To exercise these rights, or to ask us to delete your data, email tolgacebeci.studio@gmail.com with the name of the App, your platform (Android or iOS) and, if you can, your player ID or advertising ID so we can find your data. We will reply within 30 days and will forward deletion requests to the third parties listed above where they hold the data. You can also delete cloud saves stored with Google Play Games or iCloud from your Google or Apple account settings.

You have the right to complain to your local data protection authority, or in Türkiye to the Personal Data Protection Authority (Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurumu).


8. International Transfers


We are based in Türkiye, and our third-party partners process information in the United States, the European Union and other countries. Where information is transferred out of Türkiye, the EEA or the UK, we rely on the safeguards required by applicable law, such as standard contractual clauses, or on your explicit consent where the law requires it.


9. Children's Privacy


Our Apps are intended for users aged 13 and over and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has used an App and provided personal information, please contact us and we will delete it and ask our partners to do the same.


10. Security


Information is transmitted using encrypted connections, and we work only with partners that apply industry-standard security measures. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.


11. Links to Other Sites


The Apps and advertisements in them may contain links to other sites and services that we do not operate. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices, so please review their privacy policies.


12. Changes to This Privacy Policy


We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above. If a change materially affects how we use your information, we will ask for your consent again where the law requires it.
