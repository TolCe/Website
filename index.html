Colony: Core Defence

Merge pods, charge the Tesla Core, hold the line against an alien swarm.

Merge-defence with roguelike runs. Pods placed around a central Tesla Core are charged by rotating emitters, then auto-deploy units into battle. Merge identical pods to level up; max-level pods unlock a giant Prime unit.

  • 27 units across 6 rarities, 8 Tesla emitters
  • 60+ alien enemies & bosses, 10 worlds, 100+ levels
  • Roguelike card picks between waves; in-run level-up perks
  • Link Patterns: merges buff neighbouring pods
  • Meta: unit-card upgrades, relics, modules, Endless mode

Caravan Business

Buy low, sell high and guard your caravan in turn-based hex battles.

A trading sim fused with tactical combat. Load wagons, pick routes and trade across the realm of Fartherhost, where prices shift daily and react to your own trades. Bandits and wolves are fought in turn-based hex battles.

  • Dynamic, player-reactive market economy
  • Build a caravan from many animals & mercenary types
  • Manage food, wages and morale on the road
  • 5 factions with their own laws and rivals
  • Gossip & informants, banking, smuggling, smithy crafting from loot

Foto Fishing: Odyssey

Dive into 9 ocean regions and photograph 58 sea creatures for your field guide.

A fishing game where the catch is a photo. Players explore reefs, kelp forests, night waters, ice shelves and the Abyssal Trench, and every shot is scored on framing, focus and fish behaviour.

  • 9 regions, 45 dive sites, each with a mission & 3-star rating
  • 58-species field guide to complete
  • Upgrade 5 cameras, baits and strobe gear
  • Gold + research-point dual progression
  • Localised: English, Turkish, German, Spanish, French

Match Flow

Two puzzles, one screen: match-3 below, pixel art to break apart above.

Matches on the bottom match-3 board send colours up a conveyor that blasts same-coloured blocks out of a pixel-art picture. Every swap costs a move, so each level is a planning puzzle.

  • Rocket (4 in a row) and 3×3 bomb (5 / L / T) power-ups
  • Chainable power-up combos
  • 90+ hand-crafted pixel-art levels
  • Move-limited levels for tight difficulty tuning
  • Auto-save progress

Mall Security

Every level is a different job for the mall's security guard.

A collection of quick, one-hand mini-missions built around a single fantasy: keeping the mall safe. Variety per level keeps sessions fresh while a shared progression ties them together.

  • Scan bags for smugglers, compare bags to receipts
  • Watch CCTV to catch thieves, chase runners
  • Patrol parking, inspect food stands, run gate checks
  • Pursue getaway cars; back a truck into the dock
  • Unlock higher-rank guards & police cars; XP, coins, gems
